Pre-Season Indoor Practice Template

⏱️ Duration: 60 Minutes

🏟️ Space: 60' × 100' Indoor Turf / Cages

🎯 Focus: High Repetition, Fundamentals, Efficiency

0:00 - 0:10 | Dynamic Warm-Up & Mobility

Goal: Prepare muscles for explosive movements and prevent injury.

  • Jogs: High knees, butt kicks, carioca, shuffling (2 mins)
  • Dynamic Stretches: Walking lunges with twist, frankensteins, inchworms (3 mins)
  • Arm Care (Baseball/Softball): Band work, Jaeger sports routine, short toss (5 mins)

0:10 - 0:25 | Station Rotation A (Skill Isolation)

Divide into 3 groups. 5 minutes per station.

Station 1: Tee Work / Soft Toss (Cage 1)

Focus on mechanics, load, and stride. 10 reps, switch.

Station 2: Ground Balls (Turf Zone A)

Kneeling rolled balls to forehand, backhand, and center. Focus on glove presentation.

Station 3: Agility & Conditioning (Turf Zone B)

Ladder drills, cone shuttles, short sprints. Keep intensity high.

0:25 - 0:40 | Station Rotation B (Game Situations)

Keep same groups. 5 minutes per station.

Station 1: Live BP or Front Toss (Cage 2)

Situational hitting: Hit behind the runner, opposite field approach.

Station 2: Double Plays / Footwork (Turf Zone A)

Middle infielders: turns and flips. Corner infielders: slow rollers and throwing mechanics.

Station 3: Baserunning (Turf Zone B)

Leads, explosive first steps, rounding bases efficiently.

0:40 - 0:55 | Team Drill / Live Scrimmage

Bring the whole team together.

Option 1: Infield/Outfield Execution

Run defensive plays with runners on base. Emphasize communication and cuts/relays.

Option 2: Mini-Scrimmage (Modified)

Pitcher vs. Hitter in the main cage, with defense spread in the turf area (if safe/applicable). Focus on game-speed decision making.

0:55 - 1:00 | Cool Down & Debrief

  • Cool Down: Light jog, static stretching (hamstrings, shoulders, back).
  • Debrief: Coach highlights positive takeaways, areas for improvement, and upcoming schedule.
